# Community expectations

We welcome constructive contributions from people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, provide actionable feedback, credit others' work, avoid harassment and do not publish other people's private information.

Technical disagreements should focus on evidence, reproduction steps and maintainability rather than personal criticism. Maintainers may moderate discussions and decline contributions that violate these expectations.

Please use repository issues for ordinary project discussion; for private security or privacy matters, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
